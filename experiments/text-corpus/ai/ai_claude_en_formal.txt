Artificial intelligence continues to reshape the landscape of modern technology, influencing fields ranging from healthcare to finance. The emergence of large language models has accelerated progress in natural language processing, enabling machines to generate, translate, and summarize text with unprecedented fluency. While these advances offer significant benefits in productivity and accessibility, they also raise important questions regarding authorship, authenticity, and the potential for misuse. As these systems become more capable, the need for robust detection and verification mechanisms grows increasingly urgent, prompting researchers and policymakers alike to reconsider how trust is established in digital content.
